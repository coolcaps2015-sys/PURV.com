A diversified group built on polymers

Purv Group is a Pan India conglomerate based in Kolkata, with subsidiaries in trading, manufacturing, recycling, education and agriculture. Our flagship, Cool Caps Industries, is listed on the NSE.

We buy resin at scale, run modern machinery and recycle PET bottles back into flakes, so customers get one dependable source from raw material to finished closure.

  • Quality firstTop-grade raw materials, reputed partners and state-of-the-art machines.
  • Trusted supply partnersDistributors for Indian Oil, SRF, UFLEX, Jubilant and Podar Pigments.
  • One-stop sourcingFilms, closures, preforms and inks under one roof.
